|---|---|---|
| DSH Go brand mark (fish tail and bubbles) | `app/assets/brand/`; and the files derived from it: `app/lib/ui/shell/brand_glyph.g.dart`, launcher icons `app/android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/`, `drawable/ic_launcher_*` | DSH Go's own brand graphics |

| Whale Girl (pixel whale-girl emoji set) | Source files `app/tool/pet_src/whale-girl/` (`index.html` is the original page at https://dshtui.com/pets/whale-girl-emoji/, which contains the event mappings and per-frame durations); pet pack `app/assets/pets/whale-girl/`; the "Star" popup sticker `app/assets/star/star_whale.webp` | DSH-TUI's mascot, originally published on dshtui.com |

The whale-tail graphics (`app/assets/whale/`, source files `app/tool/whale_src/`): one of them is DeepSeek's official icon, whose copyright and trademark belong to DeepSeek and are used only to indicate that this app connects to the DeepSeek service; the others are fan works based on it. They are likewise not covered by the AGPL-3.0 license. If the rights holder asks, we will replace them.

These whale designs are DSH-TUI's own unofficial imagery; they are unaffiliated with and do not represent DeepSeek.

The go.dshtui.com home page (`site/home/`) was made by community developers ClayGao233 / Caiyunshi Shaobing; the origins and terms of use of the web page itself are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The same terms apply to its `dist/assets/dsh-go.svg` (a copy of `app/assets/brand/tile.svg`), `dist/assets/dsh-go-pop.svg` (a 3D variant derived from the brand graphics), `dist/assets/author/share.png` (a copy of `docs/assets/social-preview.png`), and to the brand graphics and whale-girl imagery in the feature showcase images.

The showcase assets used by the repository home page README, `docs/assets/` (hero image, social preview, desktop-pet GIF, community QR code), are also subject to the terms on this page: you may view and repost them along with this repository, but do not use them in products you release yourself.

Pet packs are generated by `app/tool/make_pet_pack.py` from `app/tool/pet_src/`, and the brand-mark code is generated by `app/tool/make_brand_mark.py` from `app/assets/brand/`. The tool scripts themselves, like the rest of the source, are released under AGPL-3.0.

The Companion Hut's Live2D assets are not in this repository or the APK: Cubism Core (Live2D proprietary license), the Cubism Web Framework (Live2D Open Software License) and the character model are packaged as a separate asset pack, loaded onto the phone only when used — see [docs/design/companion.md](docs/design/companion.md). The model's author and license are written in the asset pack's `character.json`, and the App displays them verbatim at the bottom of its menu.

Third-party code, fonts, icons and runtime components are listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
